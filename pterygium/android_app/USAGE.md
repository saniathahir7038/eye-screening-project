# Eye Screening APK: Quick Use Guide

## Install and open

1. Install the APK on an Android phone.
2. Open **Eye Screening**.
3. Choose **Pterygium**, **Keratoconus**, or **Dry Eye screening**.

## Pterygium screening

1. Open **Pterygium screening**.
2. Tap **Capture photo** or **Choose photo**.
3. Use a clear, close-up eye image with the full iris and visible white of the eye.
4. Wait for the on-device result.
5. Retake the photo if the application reports an image-quality problem.

## Keratoconus screening

1. Open **Keratoconus screening**.
2. Choose one of the four included SmartKC sample images.
3. Tap **Run sample test**.
4. Review the screening result, PPK, aCLMI, Sim-K values, axial map, and tangential map.

This build uses bundled sample results. Live Placido-image processing is still under development.

## Dry Eye screening

1. Attach the Placido ring and turn on its light.
2. Open **Dry Eye screening** and select the left or right eye.
3. Tap **Record Placido video**.
4. Centre the reflected rings, ask the person to blink once, and then comfortably keep the eye open.
5. Record for up to 15 seconds and stop if the person feels uncomfortable.
6. Review the video and tap **Share test video** to return it for hardware validation.

This hardware-test build records and exports the video. Automated NIBUT and Dry Eye results are still under development.

## Current result availability

| Module | Current capability |
|---|---|
| Pterygium | Live on-device screening |
| Keratoconus | Four bundled SmartKC sample tests |
| Dry Eye | Placido video capture, playback, and sharing |

This is a research screening application and does not provide a medical diagnosis.
